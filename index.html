Frequently Asked Questions

How much does interior painting cost in Lincoln, NE?

Most projects range from $1,500 to $6,000 depending on the size of the space, number of rooms, and condition of existing walls. Single-room jobs typically start around $400–$800.

How long does interior painting take?

A single room typically takes one day. A full home interior usually takes three to five days depending on prep work needed and the number of coats required.

Do I need to move my furniture before the painters arrive?

We ask that you clear small items and valuables. Our crew can help move larger furniture to the center of the room and will cover everything with drop cloths.

Are your painters licensed and insured?

Yes. Lincoln Interior Painting is fully licensed and insured. Every job is covered so you never have to worry about liability or property damage.
