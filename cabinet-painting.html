Our Cabinet Painting Process

Quality cabinet painting is all about preparation. Here's how we approach every job.

1

Cabinet Removal

We remove all cabinet doors and hardware before painting begins.

2

Cleaning & Degreasing

Kitchen cabinets accumulate grease and grime over time. We clean every surface thoroughly before any paint is applied.

3

Sanding & Priming

All surfaces are sanded and primed to ensure proper adhesion and a smooth, durable finish.

4

Professional Finish Coats

We apply durable topcoats designed specifically for cabinetry — not standard wall paint.

5

Reinstallation

Doors and hardware are reinstalled after the finish has fully cured.

6

Final Inspection

We walk through the completed project with you to make sure every door, drawer, and surface meets your expectations.

Frequently Asked Questions About Cabinet Painting

How much does cabinet painting cost in Lincoln, NE?

Most kitchen cabinet painting projects in Lincoln range from $1,200 to $4,000 depending on the number of cabinet doors, drawer fronts, and box surfaces being painted, as well as the condition of the existing finish. We provide free estimates so you know exactly what to expect before any work begins.

How long does cabinet painting take?

Most kitchen cabinet painting projects take two to three days — one day for prep and priming, one to two days for finish coats and reinstallation. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start.

What kind of paint do you use on cabinets?

We use cabinet-specific paints and primers designed for high-traffic surfaces. These products are more durable than standard wall paint and are formulated to resist chipping, scratching, and yellowing over time.

Can you paint over stained wood cabinets?

Yes. Proper preparation — including cleaning, sanding, and priming — allows us to paint over stained or previously finished wood cabinets and achieve a smooth, durable result.
