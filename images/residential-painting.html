Frequently Asked Questions About Residential Painting

How much does residential painting cost in Lincoln, NE?

Most residential interior painting projects in Lincoln range from $1,500 to $8,000 depending on the size of the home, number of rooms, ceiling height, and the condition of existing walls. Single rooms typically start around $400. We provide free, no-obligation estimates so you know exactly what to expect.

How do I prepare my home for interior painters?

We ask that you clear small items and valuables from the rooms being painted. Our crew will move larger furniture to the center of the room and cover everything with drop cloths. You don't need to do much — we handle the prep.

Are your painters licensed and insured?

Yes. Lincoln Interior Painting is fully licensed and insured. Every job is covered so you never have to worry about liability or property damage.

How long will my residential painting project take?

A single room typically takes one day. A whole-home interior usually takes three to five days depending on the scope of work. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start.
