Questions About Cost

Most interior painting projects in Lincoln range from $1,500 to $6,000 depending on the size of the space, number of rooms, ceiling height, and the condition of existing walls. Single-room jobs typically start around $400–$800. We provide free, no-obligation estimates so you know exactly what to expect before any work begins.
A whole-home interior repaint in Lincoln typically ranges from $3,000 to $8,000 depending on square footage, number of rooms, ceiling height, and the condition of existing walls. Larger homes with vaulted ceilings or significant prep work will fall toward the higher end of that range.
Most kitchen cabinet painting projects in Lincoln range from $1,200 to $4,000 depending on the number of doors, drawer fronts, and box surfaces being painted. Cabinet painting delivers a dramatic transformation at a fraction of the cost of full cabinet replacement.

Questions About Timeline

A single room typically takes one full day. A whole-home interior usually takes three to five days depending on the number of rooms, ceiling height, and the amount of prep work required. We'll give you a clear timeline before we start so you can plan accordingly.
Most kitchen cabinet painting projects take two to three days — one day for prep and priming, and one to two days for finish coats and reinstallation. We'll give you a specific timeline after seeing your kitchen.
Availability varies by season. Contact us for current scheduling availability and we'll find a start date that works for you.
